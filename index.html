Borderlands are lived worlds.

Rather than treating borders only as lines or institutions, I approach them as lived social settings shaped by movement, exchange, and everyday negotiation.

Research orientation
“My research brings together cultural anthropology, borderlands, well-being, and inequality.”

This orientation connects several distinct empirical projects across social, geographic, and digital settings.

Academic trajectory

A research path across disciplines and places.
